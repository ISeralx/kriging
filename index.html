Multi-Agent Kriging

A swarm of agents searches an unknown field for its global maximum — building a Gaussian-process (Kriging) model as they go, and balancing curiosity against greed. Move the sliders and watch it think.

In plain words: imagine hunting for the highest point of a dark, hilly landscape — but you can only feel the ground exactly where you stand. That's the problem here: find the maximum of an unknown field from a handful of point measurements. The engine is Kriging — a Gaussian-process method born in 1950s gold mining, and today standard for mapping oil & gas reservoirs from sparse wells. Pair it with a swarm that decides where to measure next, and you get how autonomous systems home in on a hidden source: a gas leak, a radiation hotspot, a mineral deposit.
